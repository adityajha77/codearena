# CodeArena Demo Script 🎬🏆
**Duration:** 3 - 4 Minutes
**Goal:** Showcase the full "Accountability Loop" from Staking to Winning.

---

### **[0:00 - 0:45] PART 1: The Problem & The Solution**
**(Visual: Open the Landing Page with the rotating particles)**

"Hello everyone! We’ve all been there—you start a coding streak, you’re motivated for three days, and then... you quit. 

Traditional platforms give you a digital badge for consistency, but they don't give you a reason *not* to fail. That’s why we built **CodeArena**. It’s a Web3 accountability layer on Solana that turns your coding habits into a high-stakes game. On CodeArena, you don't just promise to code—you put your money where your keyboard is."

### **[0:45 - 1:30] PART 2: Joining the Arena**
**(Visual: Navigate to the Challenges page and click 'Join' on a challenge)**

"Let’s see how it works. I’m joining the 'Global GitHub Streak' challenge. I have to commit code every day for 7 days. I’ll stake **0.1 SOL** into the smart contract. 

**(Visual: Sign the Phantom/Solana transaction)**

Once I sign this transaction, my SOL is locked in a secure Vault on the Solana blockchain. If I stay consistent, I get it all back. If I fail... it gets slashed. You'll notice I've linked my GitHub handle—CodeArena will now verify my activity every day in real-time."

---

### **[1:30 - 2:15] PART 3: Daily Verification & The "Police"**
**(Visual: Show an Active Challenge Card with the 'Verify' button and the timer)**

"Every day, I have 24 hours to solve a problem. Here is my active challenge dashboard. I’ve just made a commit on GitHub, so let’s verify.

**(Visual: Click 'Verify Progress'. Show the green 'Solved for Today' state)**

The system checks the GitHub API, confirms my work, and protects my SOL for another 24 hours. But what happens if I forget? 

**(Visual: Show the Telegram Bot sending a reminder)**

That’s where the **CodeArena Bot** comes in. Our backend scheduler monitors every participant 24/7. It sends me a Telegram reminder when I have 3 hours left. If I still miss the goal, the backend automatically triggers an on-chain slash, taking 50% of my stake."
